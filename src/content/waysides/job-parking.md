You keep the field gate shut while dogs are running and get cars parked where
they belong. The gate is the important half.

## The gate

Closed whenever a dog is on the course. No exceptions, no quick ones, no
holding it for someone who is nearly there. A dog chasing a lure is running
flat out and is not watching for an open gate, and a dog that gets out of the
field is the worst thing that can happen at a trial.

Watch the start line rather than the gate. When a run finishes and the next
has not started, that is your window.

## Parking

Point people to the dedicated area rather than letting them pick. Cars parked
tidily at the start of the day save an hour of shuffling later, and they keep
the space clear for the people who arrive last.

## Cars leaving

Same rule as the gate. Nobody moves while a dog is running. People start
packing up as soon as their dog has finished, so most of this comes in the
afternoon, and most of it is telling someone to wait ninety seconds.

## What you don't need

Any prior experience, and no knowledge of the sport at all. You need to be
willing to say no to someone in a car who is in a hurry.

## What it's like

Outdoors and on your feet, at the edge of the field rather than in the middle
of it. Bring a coat, because you will be standing still.
